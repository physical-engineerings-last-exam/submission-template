**Acceptance basis and limits.** The basis is Article 3.4.B, using Vantree's modified Proctor test P04 (ASTM D1557 Method C): maximum dry density 128.4 pcf at 9.2% optimum moisture. That gives two limits, and both must be met at every test location:
- dry density of at least 122.0 pcf;
- moisture content between 8.2% and 11.2%.

The spec also requires at least three tests on this 11,400 sf lift.

**What the record establishes, and what it doesn't.** The criterion itself is solidly established: Articles 3.4.B and 3.4.D, sheet S-201 Rev.4, the geotechnical report, and the EOR's confirmation under register item 027. The P04 values are not established for this fill:
- P04 was tested on Larchwood Pit material.
- The Contractor's placement log says this fill came from the stockpile north of Block C. RFI 079 accepted that stockpile only "subject to gradation retest."
- There is no Proctor test, gradation or source identification on file for the stockpile.

So the P04 limits can support a rejection but not an acceptance.

**Results**
- **SF-26-0708 used the wrong reference.** It used a standard Proctor (D698) on the Phase 1 Wexford Ridge borrow, a silty clay, which Article 1.2.A.1 excludes. It also checked density only. Its "PASS" results carry no weight under the section.
- **Against P04:** FD-14 (6.1%) and FD-17 (5.8%) fail on moisture. FD-15 and FD-16 meet both limits but can't be accepted until the correct reference for the placed material exists.
- **The rejection holds whatever the correct reference turns out to be.** The moisture readings span 5.8% to 10.4%, wider than the 3-point window, so at least two locations fail under any optimum.

- there is no reference test on the material actually placed;

- the tester left at 11:30 while placement and grading ran until 17:00;
