**Why it doesn't verify** (all checked against the project record):
1. **Toe embedment.** Rev.2 uses an undrained shear strength (su) of 1,400 psf. The geotechnical report gives 900–1,100 psf, and the design basis memo says the report governs. On the logged values the factor of safety (FS) is 0.31–0.81 against 1.50 required. Kesterline's own check only passes if su is at least 1,378 psf. In the long-term drained condition the clay gives no net toe resistance at all.
2. **Tieback anchors.** At 20° with a 25 ft free and 24 ft bonded length, the bonded zone sits at El. 737.0–728.7, entirely in the clay. The 20 psi bond stress used is a sand-and-gravel value. Limited by the clay's strength, each anchor can carry at most 33.9–41.5 kips. The design load is 56.2 kips and the proof load 74.8 kips, so the anchors would fail proof testing.
3. **Base heave.** The arithmetic (FS 1.60) is right, but:
   - the stop-work trigger at El. 727.0 still lets FS drop to 1.48;
   - if pumping is lost at subgrade, FS is 0.58;
   - the relief-well system has no supporting design;
   - no Block B dewatering plan exists, and the Block A plan explicitly excludes Block B.
4. **Rev.2's supersession table points to the wrong Rev.1 sections.** As a result it leaves Rev.1's dry-excavation assumption and its El. 722.0 toe in force.
5. **Location.** Drawing S-101 Rev.4 puts the pit inside the completed basement mat (grids H–L / 3–5), with the southernmost row of piles on its north edge. Kesterline designed an open cut from El. 750.0 and states there are no structures nearby.
