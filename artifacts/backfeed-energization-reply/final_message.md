# Agent's final message

I've drafted the reply letter and opened one OpenProject work package (#47). The letter tells Ms. Strathe that, as the records stand at 07/14/2025, we cannot energize Site 3 on 07/29/2025 or confirm that date.

**Why the date can't be confirmed**
1. **The Township hasn't given final approval.** Kelloway Township permit EP-25-0418 says "No permitted work shall be energized before final approval." The backfeed would energize exactly that work, and the final inspection isn't until 07/24/2025. The backfeed term in the agreement lets us energize at her written request, but it doesn't override the permit.
2. **Five things in Tamsworth's construction drawings don't meet the 2023 National Electrical Code (NEC)** that the permit cites:
   - **Panel and tracker-controller mounts:** AUX-1 and the eight tracker controllers sit outdoors on enamel-painted steel strut (NEC 300.6(A)(1)).
   - **Tracker drive wiring:** the 96 tracker drive circuits are Type TC cable run along the row piles, with no tray, conduit or support wire (NEC 336.12).
   - **Skid cable:** the inverter output circuits on the skids are Type USE-2 cable above ground (NEC 338.12(B)(2)). This one can be closed by showing the cable is also rated RHW-2.
   - **Main breaker:** the switchboard's 2,000 A main breaker carries 1,660 A of continuous inverter output, so the code calls for 2,075 A (NEC 705.30(B)). Showing the breaker is rated for 100% continuous load would also close this one.
   - **Missing labels:** the switchboard has no "fed by multiple sources" warning label and there is no power-source directory (NEC 705.12(B)(3)(3) and 705.10).

   The first three are on equipment the backfeed itself would energize; the last two matter mainly once the plant runs in parallel. Everything else I checked in the drawings holds up, including wire and breaker sizes elsewhere, grounding conductors and fault ratings.

If those aren't in by 07/29, the backfeed moves to a new date. The letter also restates that the inverters stay disabled throughout.
